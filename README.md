## Course Platforms for Creators

- [Podia](https://podia.com/) - Platform for creators to sell online courses, digital downloads, and memberships. Features no transaction fees on paid plans. ([Read more](/details/podia.md)) `Paid` `API` `Creators`
- [Teachable](https://teachable.com/) - Platform for creators to create and sell online courses and coaching services. It handles payments, hosting, and student management. ([Read more](/details/teachable.md)) `Freemium` `Paid` `API` `Creators`
- [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/) - Online course platform for entrepreneurs and creators to build and sell digital products. It includes website building and marketing tools. ([Read more](/details/thinkific.md)) `Freemium` `Paid` `API` `Creators`

## LMS for Schools & Universities

- [Blackboard Learn](https://www.blackboard.com/) - LMS for higher education and K-12 institutions. Provides course delivery, assessment, and analytics tools. ([Read more](/details/blackboard-learn.md)) `Paid` `Enterprise` `API` `Education`
- [Canvas LMS](https://www.instructure.com/canvas) - Cloud-based learning management system for higher education and K-12 institutions. It offers course management, analytics, and mobile access. ([Read more](/details/canvas-lms.md)) `Paid` `API` `Education` `Cloud`
- [D2L Brightspace](https://www.d2l.com/) - LMS for K-12, higher education, and enterprise. Offers accessibility features and data analytics. ([Read more](/details/d2l-brightspace.md)) `Paid` `Enterprise` `API` `Education`
- [LearnDash](https://www.learndash.com/) - WordPress LMS plugin used by schools and businesses to create online courses. It offers quizzes, certificates, and content dripping. ([Read more](/details/learndash.md)) `Paid` `API` `WordPress` `Education`

## Open-Source LMS

- [Chamilo](https://chamilo.org/) - Open-source e-learning and collaboration system for schools and businesses. It is lightweight and easy to install. ([Read more](/details/chamilo.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `Education`
- [ILIAS](https://www.ilias.de/) - Open-source LMS for universities, schools, and enterprises. Offers authoring tools, testing, and communication features. ([Read more](/details/ilias.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `Education`
- [Moodle](https://moodle.org/) - Open-source learning platform used by educators and administrators to create personalized learning environments. It supports a wide range of plugins and is community-driven. ([Read more](/details/moodle.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `API` `Education`
